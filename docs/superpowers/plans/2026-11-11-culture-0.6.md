# 0.5 leftovers and 0.6 Culture Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Finish what 0.5 left for 0.6 (the culture mind writes laws, rituals and myths) and build 0.6 Culture: cultures, religions, the player seen as a god, and a written chronicle with heroes and named ages.

**Architecture:** Everything a mind writes stays a small JSON record forced by a grammar and validated by the engine, as inventions are. `LocalModel.Generate` and `AskModel` take the grammar / schema of the record asked for. Each kingdom carries a `Culture` (values, customs, style, religion, feelings about the player). A procedural writer always answers when no model does.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4 .NET, C# simulation (`sim/`, partial class `Simulation`), GDScript interface (`scripts/ui/hud.gd`), LLamaSharp.

**Spec:** `docs/ROADMAP.md` (0.5 last item, 0.6), `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` § 11, 12, 16, 21.

## Global Constraints

- The simulation never waits for a model; every record has a procedural fallback.
- Nothing a mind writes enters the world unvalidated.
- English in code, comments, docs and in-game text.
- Tests: `dotnet build`, then `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/test_world.gd` (exit code = failed checks).

## Review Focus

- A power dragged over a village every frame must not flood the chronicle or the memories (one memory per kind per season).
- A kingdom that falls or secedes while a model answer is pending: the answer is dropped, not applied to another kingdom.
- Custom effects are negative as well as positive: village bonuses must stay within sane bounds.
- A new world (`Start`) clears cultures, annals, chapters and ages.
- Free text from a model (names, myths, chapters): length and characters checked before it is shown.

### Task 2: Cultures (`sim/Culture.cs`)

**Interfaces — produces:**
- `Kingdom.Culture` (class `Culture`): `float[] Values` (8: war, honour, nature, wealth, knowledge, faith, freedom, tradition), `Structure`, `Nature`, `Roof`, `Walls`, `Ornament`, `List<Custom> Customs`, `Faith`, religion and god fields (Task 3).
- The custom language: `{"name","kind": law|ritual|taboo,"about": one of the 8 values,"text","effects":[{"do","job"?,"amount"}]}`. Blocks and caps: attack 15, defense 15, food 5, loyalty 10, faith 10, work_speed 15 (job), yield 20 (job), aggression 20. Amounts are whole, non-zero, within ±cap. 1–2 effects. Net power (sum of amount ÷ cap, aggression not counted) at most 0.6. A taboo has at least one negative effect. At most 3 customs of each kind: the oldest gives way.
- `string SubmitCustomJson(int k, string json, string mind = "model")` ("" when accepted), `static string ValidateCustom(CustomDraft d)`, `void CultureNow(int k)`, `Godot.Collections.Array CultureTable()`, `string CultureGrammar()`, `string CultureSchema()`.
- Effects reach villages through `RefreshMods`; `Aggression(k)` replaces the race's raw aggression in diplomacy; similar values raise `Opinion`.
- A kingdom born of a secession inherits its mother's culture and gods, drifted.

- [ ] `test_culture`: invalid customs rejected (unknown kind, effect over cap, too strong, taboo without a price, bad name, not JSON); a valid law changes `VillageMods`; the fourth law replaces the first; the procedural writer's customs are all valid; a seceding kingdom shares its mother's gods.

### Task 3: Religions and the player as a god (`sim/Religion.cs`)

**Interfaces — produces:**
- `void Remember(int k, string kind, string text, float weight)`: what a kingdom lived through (war, peace, conquest, king's death, city, fire, hero, miracle, wrath, invention). Kinds map to god domains.
- Myth record `{"title","text"}`, written about the kingdom's heaviest memory; the engine attaches the god (a new one when the domain has none, up to 5).
- `Culture.Faith` 0..100, moved each season; it adds loyalty.
- `void GodActs(string kind, float x, float y, float radius, int killed)` called by the powers: gratitude and dread per kingdom, `Stance` unnoticed | worshipped | feared | hated, the player's name in their language, at most one memory per kind and season.
- `Godot.Collections.Array ReligionTable()`, `string SubmitMythJson(int k, string json, string mind = "model")`.

- [ ] `test_culture`: blessing a village's people makes its kingdom worship the player; meteors make it fear or hate; a myth names a real event; dragging a power logs one event.

### Task 4: Chronicle, heroes and ages (`sim/Chronicle.cs`)

**Interfaces — produces:**
- Every `LogEvent` also goes to permanent annals (year, category, text).
- Heroes: `UnitManager.Kills`; a warrior's 4th kill in war makes a hero (event category "heroes", a memory).
- A chapter every 5 years: `{"title","text","age"}` from the period's events, by the model or the procedural chronicler.
- Ages: the dominant kind of events over the last 10 years names the age ("The Age of Ash"); it changes when the theme changes and the age is at least 10 years old.
- `Godot.Collections.Array ChronicleTable()`, `string AgeName()`, `string SubmitChapterJson(string json, string mind = "model")`, `void WriteChapterNow()`.

- [ ] `test_culture`: a chapter is written from events of its years; a period of wars is named an age of war; a fake model server's chapter is accepted and a broken one falls back.
